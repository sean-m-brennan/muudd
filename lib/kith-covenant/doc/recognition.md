# Two layers of recognition

A community becomes a member of a compact by some act, and stays one by some
other. What are the two acts?

The obvious answer is a joint ceremony, with the communities reciting a shared
statement together. That answer fails against the historical evidence. Joint
thick ceremonies do not work across unshared substrates, and every attempt to
run one is an intra-community mechanism applied at the wrong layer. Ask a Muslim
community and a Buddhist community to recite something together and you get
either a statement so thin it binds nobody or a statement one of them cannot
honestly say.

So we run recognition in two layers that do different work, and that split is
the central design decision of this chapter.

The *intra-community layer* is where the real binding happens. Each community
performs its own authorization rite, in its own substrate, on its own envoys and
machines. Speaking a commitment in front of one's own people is costly, and it
works in a language that community can read for sincerity.

The *inter-community layer* is a thin procedural grammar in the tradition of
Kadesh, the merchant law, and the Geneva framework, being credentialed envoys,
vouching by existing members, and an audit commons recording conduct over time.

The reason for the split is exact. Procedure and cryptography can carry the
inter-community layer. They cannot carry the intra-community layer, because
asymmetric psychological cost is a property of bodies in rooms, and you cannot
automate it.

## The five movements

The internal rite has a five-movement structure, and the community performs it
for itself rather than as a joint ritual with strangers.

Firstly, *the naming of the substrate*, in the terms of that community. Second,
*the acknowledgment of fallenness*. Third, *the recognition of the other as
peers*, bearing the *imago Dei* or the equivalent that tradition carries.
Fourth, *the commitment to the common virtues as dispositions* rather than as
rules. And lastly, *the submission of the compact itself*, which is not the
ultimate.

That last movement is the one doing structural work. It is where the second rule
of the previous chapter becomes an act somebody performs rather than a claim
somebody makes, and it is what forecloses the compact ever becoming its own
object of devotion.

The movements carry their full content one tier down, inside a community, where
a polity conducts them for each of its own members. What crosses a compact
boundary is only the attestation that the rite happened.

Asymmetric psychological cost lives here. The body knows when the words are
insincere, and members socialized in a tradition can detect hollowing in their
own people faster than any outsider can.

That mechanism is real, and its limits deserve naming here rather than in a
footnote. The claim does not rest on humans being good one-shot lie detectors,
because they are not. It rests on deception being *costly to sustain over time*.
That cost is weakest exactly where the compact needs it most, meaning in
low-contact, low-repetition, low-entanglement dealings across interplanetary
distance.

## The thin grammar between communities

Across compact boundaries the mechanism is much thinner, and deliberately so. It
has three parts.

*Credentialing* is cryptographic proof that the sending community authorized its
envoy under its own substrate. The compact does not second-guess how that
binding was done.

*Vouching* is an existing member standing for a new entrant, with its own
standing riding on the later conduct of that entrant.

*The audit commons* is an append-only record of the standing and conduct of each
member over time.

This layer assumes nothing about what happens inside the rite of a community. It
cares only that the rite happened, that the community attests to it, and that
subsequent conduct is consistent with what was said.

## The gate, and why it is the slowest surface to defend

Admissions is where a compact is most patiently attacked, because steering who
gets in is the *legitimate* path to assembling a coordinated faction past the
threshold at which the machinery becomes that faction's instrument. Nothing
about that attack looks like an attack while it is happening.

Four measures harden it, and each one closes a specific route.

*Probation withholds weight.* A probationary member carries no deliberative
weight in admissions, in mediator selection, or in designation procedures. This
one matters more than it looks, and measuring it is what showed why. Probation
only ever opened when somebody staked standing on an entrant, so a faction that
never vouched for anyone seated five of five candidates without touching the
mechanism at all.

*An admission into a standing compact needs an admitting quorum* of sitting
non-probationary members, derived from the archive rather than from a status
list maintained beside it. A status list checked against other status lists
reproduces their omissions, which is a general lesson worth carrying. Derive the
roll from the record, never from a summary of it.

*Every entrant needs a sponsor*, and the standing of that sponsor rides on what
the entrant subsequently does. Vouching has to cost something or it is not
vouching.

*Examining seats are term-limited, rotated, and pool-share capped*, sharing one
implementation with the observer caps of a later chapter. A community that could
hold a majority of examining seats holds the gate.

## Probationary weight, and how far it reaches

One question stayed open for a long time and is worth recording, because the
purposive reading is not the obvious one.

Probationary weight carries no second-class quorum. A probationary member is not
counted at a discount somewhere else. It is simply not counted in the procedures
above.

The asymmetric contraction bars had to count *weight* rather than heads, or the
extra vote that makes contraction harder than expansion collapses back to the
ordinary quorum the moment probationary members are on the roll. That is the
kind of interaction that only shows up when the rules are measured against each
other rather than read one at a time.

## Staying a member

Recognition is not a one-time act, and the machinery that keeps it live is the
same machinery that makes hollowing detectable.

Membership carries a periodic re-affirmation, and re-affirmation is a recitation
rather than a signature. A community that has amended what it commits to, and
never re-recited, becomes visible at its next cycle, because the recitation
names commitments that are no longer the ones in force.

Ceremony cost is tracked. Rites that have decayed into ritual without engagement
are detectable by attendance patterns, by the comportment of witnesses, and by
substantive response during re-affirmation. No one of those is proof, and taken
together they distinguish a community that has stopped meaning it from one that
has not.

The deepest threat the compact faces is exactly here, and it resists procedural
countermeasures better than anything else in this book. The forms persist while
the substance erodes. Substrate-deference is an appearance instrument, and the
most skilled actor games it rather than the sincerest. We state that in the
chapter on limits and do not soften it here.
