# Substitution: the object the compact stops enforcing

## Thesis

A compact can stop being what it says it is in three ways, and it has instruments for two
of them. It can weaken, which is decay, and the absolute signals of the erosion audit catch
it. It can be seized, which is capture, and the seven detectors of the capture harness catch
that. The third way keeps every form and intensifies it. The recited object goes untouched
in the record, the observances rise, the witness counts rise, and what the compact actually
enforces quietly narrows to a fraction of what it recites.

This is substitution, and it can be entirely sincere. Nobody need lie, coordinate, or
benefit. It belongs beside decay rather than inside the capture harness for that reason, and
it is the second finding of its kind in
[Erosion legibility](../architecture/erosion-legibility.md), whose first was that the
erosion alert cannot see omission. This is the sibling failure. **The erosion alert could
not see intensification either**, and for the mirror-image reason: every dimension it
carried was satisfied by a polity performing each form harder every year.

## The three modes

Decay is form and object weakening together. The rites lapse, the offices empty, the
mediator pool takes no disputes and says nothing about taking none. It is the easy case to
state and the hard case to measure, since a polity that has stopped offers no act to read,
which is why the signals that catch it are absolute and not relative.

Capture is a faction taking the machinery. It is the easy case to measure and the hard case
to state, since capture is a claim about intent, and intent is exactly what a protocol
cannot read. The capture harness resolves that by measuring traces rather than motives.
Coordination is a kind of action and leaves marks, so concentration, divergence, and
correlated behaviour are readable where the purpose behind them is not.

Substitution is neither. The form is kept, and kept harder than before. The object is
replaced. A compact founded on eight grounds finds, over a generation, that it enforces one
of them, and it enforces that one with rising vigour against a widening set of members. No
amendment records the change, because no amendment was proposed. Nobody narrowed the
recitation. The recitation is still read in full at every rite, and the community that
recites it means it.

The clearest way to see the difference is what each does to the numbers. Decay makes volume
fall, coverage fall, and the spread of targets fall together, since a polity doing less does
less of everything. Capture makes volume rise while enforcement concentrates on the parties
the faction has an interest in. Substitution makes volume rise while coverage falls and the
targets stay dispersed, which is the combination neither of the other two produces.

## Two demonologies, and the gap between them

The corpus already carries two accounts of how a community goes wrong, and the five
anti-spiral features stack a countermeasure from each of them.

Girard's account is of the sacrificial mechanism, and the countermeasure is revelation.
Scapegoating is disarmed by being seen as scapegoating, so the third anti-spiral feature is
an exposure requirement. Although the assumption behind it is sound wherever there is
something anomalous to display, the countermeasure reaches only what can be shown, named,
and recognised as what it is.

Wink's account is of the Powers, structures that are not evil in their creation and become
so by claiming ultimacy for themselves, and the countermeasure is renunciation rather than
exposure. The fifth anti-spiral feature is that renunciation, which is why the corpus
insists it be demonstrated in practice rather than asserted in a founding document. A
compact that declares non-ultimacy and acts in every operational moment as though it were
the ultimate has satisfied the assertion and failed the feature.

Substitution falls in the gap between them. Revelation does not fire, because nothing
anomalous is on display. There is no innocent victim being singled out for the compact to
recognise, no crisis, no purge. There is a rising quantity of ordinary enforcement, filed
correctly, on a ground that genuinely is part of the recited object. Renunciation does not
fire either, because the compact has not claimed ultimacy for itself. It has kept its
compact-incapable list intact, held its fasts, and refused every matter it undertook to
refuse. What it has done is narrow the ground it stands on without saying so, and neither
countermeasure was built to see a narrowing that arrives fully dressed as fidelity.

## Why the instruments read substitution as health

Every count-based instrument the compact owns reads a substituting polity as improving,
because on the counts it is improving.

The ceremony signals ask when a community last sealed a rite and whether the latest rite
drew fewer witnesses than its founding. A substituting polity seals ahead of cadence with
witness counts that climb, so both read clean and the second reads better than clean. The
constitutional signals ask whether a matter has been removed from the compact-incapable
list, whether a forbearance has been narrowed out of the adopted ground, whether a fast has
been shortened, and whether anyone deliberated during one. All four require an act, and no
act occurs. The function signals ask whether a standing function has gone silent or a seat
has gone empty. Neither has. The deliberation signal asks whether the latest mediation
answered fewer arguments than the first. It did not.

Although the capture detectors are aimed at a different failure entirely, they are worse
placed still, for the reason the uniform-drift audit already gave about them. They read
concentration, divergence, and correlation, and a polity that moves as a body offers none
of the three. There is no faction here, so there is no cohort for one to diverge from.

This was measured before the dimension was built rather than argued for. Three compacts of
eight communities each, thirty enforcement acts apiece, rites ahead of cadence with rising
witness counts: one enforcing a single ground against five different members, one enforcing
all eight, and one enforcing a single ground against a single member. All three scored
`None` with no reasons on the erosion alert, and identically on substrate deference,
collusive cohorts, and observer pool concentration. Nothing the compact owned distinguished
a polity that had swapped its object from one that had not.

## The measure

While the first design assumed a new record would be needed, the record already carried
everything, and the correction is worth stating. Every conduct record names the ground it
bears on, in a fixed taxonomy of five anti-spiral features plus the commons obligation,
charity, and rescue, with an open `Other` for anything outside it. Every record names its
subject, its filers, and the standpoint the filers declared, and an enforcement act is
distinguished from an observation by carrying a position on the escalation ladder. No
format changed, no schema moved, and no signature scheme was touched.

The reading is coverage over that taxonomy, taken across a window, alongside how widely the
enforcement is spread over subjects. A converging compact is one filing enough acts to read
at all, citing too little of the taxonomy, across too many members for the concentration to
be about any of them.

Two exclusions turned out to matter, and both were found by building rather than by
planning. Substantive re-engagement sits on the escalation ladder and is not a dispute rung.
It is the track a community returns along, so counting it as enforcement would read the most
hopeful record the compact keeps as one of its harshest. And the open `Other` ground is
counted, reported, and deliberately kept out of the denominator, which turns out to need no
rule of its own. A compact filing everything off the shared taxonomy scores zero coverage
and fires on the ordinary reading, while one genuinely novel ground among a covered taxonomy
moves the reading not at all.

## Why the denominator must be fixed

The uniform-drift audit established that only absolute measures see sincere drift, because a
relative measure moves with the thing it is measuring. Coverage over the ground taxonomy is
absolute by construction, since the denominator is a fixed enumeration written into the
protocol rather than a summary of what the compact has been doing lately. A compact cannot
improve its coverage by enforcing less.

That is also why the open ground stays outside it. A denominator that grew every time a
filer invented a category would let the compact supply its own reference, and a compact that
had narrowed to one invented ground would score perfect coverage of a taxonomy of one.

The floor beneath the reading is arithmetic rather than taste. Coverage over eight grounds
is a coupon-collector quantity, so a small number of acts is expected to touch only some of
the grounds however evenly a compact is enforcing. Twelve acts drawn evenly across eight
grounds are expected to reach about six of them, a coverage near four fifths and clear of
the half-taxonomy floor. Eight acts reach about five and a half, a coverage of two thirds,
close enough that ordinary variation in a small young compact would trip the reading. Below
the floor the reading reports untestable, which is a different answer from healthy and is
recorded as such.

## What separates substitution from a recurring problem

A compact with one genuinely difficult member shows high volume and low coverage too. It
files repeatedly, on the same ground, because that is the ground the member keeps failing.
Coverage cannot tell the two apart, and a reading built on coverage alone would report a
compact for having a persistent offender.

The separation is where the enforcement lands. Legitimate concentration lands on a target,
and substitution concentrates on a ground across many targets. The dispersion of subjects is
therefore not a decoration on the reading but the discriminator, and dropping it would leave
a measure that fires on the honest case as readily as on the substituting one. The negative
control is built accordingly: two compacts identical in volume and identical in coverage,
separated only by whether the enforcement lands on one member or on five, with the flag
required on the second and silence required on the first.

A second control corrected the design itself. The natural place to take this reading is per
community, and it is the wrong place. Six communities each filing on one ground apiece score
as converged on every single per-community reading, while the compact between them still
enforces six of the eight grounds. That is division of labour in an observer corps and not a
narrowing of what the compact holds. The reading that grades the compact is therefore
compact-wide, and the per-community reading is the drill-down that names who, available only
after the compact-wide reading has said there is anything to name.

## Where the enforcement lands

The narrowed ground does not land evenly, which is the Girardian half of the reading and the
one place the two demonologies do meet. A mechanism that selects victims prefers those with
no avengers, and a compact has a name for reduced standing already. Probation is the
compact's own second class, so the direction of enforcement is readable without any new
record: acts aimed at members holding reduced standing, acts aimed at members holding full
standing, and acts between equals, with each party's standing resolved at the instant the act
was recorded rather than at the moment of reading.

This is reported as its own dimension and not folded into the first, because either can
occur without the other. It is also thresholded rather than absolute, and the reason is that
some downward skew is what probation exists for. A compact that scrutinises its entrants
more closely than its founders is doing what the admissions rules ask of it. A compact whose
enforcement has stopped reaching full members at all is doing something else, and the
negative control for the dimension is a compact enforcing against full members at any
volume, which must never fire.

## The compact's own denominator

The shared taxonomy is the denominator the *protocol* supplies. The stronger reading grades
a polity against the commitments it recited in its own rite, so that the denominator is the
object that polity holds rather than the one every polity shares, and it is now built.

It had to wait on protocol design rather than on a lens. Movement 4 carried the common
virtue as an opaque block of text with no indexable structure at any layer, so there was no
way to cite the third commitment of a recited list because there was no list. The movement
now carries a preamble and an ordered list of commitments, each a key and the text recited
for it, with the spoken form derived from the list rather than stored beside it so that the
prose and the list cannot disagree. A conduct record carries the keys it is about, next to
and not instead of its bearing: the bearing says which shared ground an act touches and the
keys say which undertaking.

The reason the finer reading is worth its cost is that eight grounds are coarse. A compact
that quietly stops holding hospitality to strangers while keeping charity to members moves
`Charity` coverage not at all. Measured before the reading existed: a compact that halved
what it recited across three generational rites, with rising witness counts and nothing
amended, scored a **perfect** 1.0 on shared-taxonomy coverage at volume 32 and graded
`None` on every one of the fourteen erosion dimensions the audit then had.

**The denominator is pinned to the first rite of each member**, which is what keeps a
per-polity measure absolute rather than relative. A compact cannot raise its coverage by
reciting a shorter list, because the denominator is what it undertook and not what it still
says. The union only grows: an admitted community adds its commitments and no departure
record exists to take any away, so the reading gets harder as a compact ages and cannot be
improved by any act of the compact at all. A commitment taken on later is counted and
reported outside the object, on the same principle that keeps the open ground out of the
shared denominator.

The move that would make a narrowing legal is to bring the recitation down to match the
enforcement, and the pinned denominator does not see that by construction, so a second
reading does: the commitments a community undertook at its first rite and no longer
recites. It compares the pinned baseline against the latest recitation rather than tallying
every drop, so a commitment taken up again clears while the archive still carries both
rites.

Two calibrations carry the rest. The volume floor **scales with the object**, because
coverage over `n` commitments is a coupon-collector quantity and here `n` varies per
compact, so a constant floor would report the compact that undertook the most as narrowing
forever. And testability counts only the acts that cite something. That correction came
from an existing control rather than from a plan: counting uncited acts read a compact
prosecuting one real adversary through thirteen acts that named no commitment as enforcing
none of its object, which measures adoption of a citation habit rather than the object, and
it fired on the most sympathetic case there is. Silence about the object is not evidence
about the object, and what the silence costs is reported as an uncited share instead.

The larger limit is the one the uniform-drift audit already states about all of this work,
and it is not softened here. No dimension prevents substitution. A compact whose members
have all quietly agreed on the smaller object will read the alert and see a compact in
excellent health being nagged by an instrument nobody calibrated for their situation, and
they will be sincere about that too. What the measure buys is that the narrowing is on the
record, dated, and reconstructable by a later reader who wants to know when the object
changed, who narrowed it, and what it was before. That is forensic legibility for the
remnant rather than prevention, which is the
only thing the architecture has ever claimed.

## Open questions

- Whether the window is the right shape at all. A trailing window asks what the compact
  enforces now, and a compact that narrows and then broadens again looks identical to one
  that never narrowed. A reading against the founding decade would see the difference and
  would also report every compact that legitimately changed its emphasis over a generation.
- What the coverage floor should be for a compact whose taxonomy is genuinely unbalanced.
  Rescue is inviolable and rare, so a compact may go decades without an occasion to enforce
  it, and a floor that expects even coverage across grounds of unequal frequency is
  measuring the frequency of occasions rather than the fidelity of the compact.
- Whether the direction reading survives contact with a compact that admits members in
  cohorts. A large intake puts many members in probation at once, so enforcement that is
  simply proportionate to how many members are new would read as running downward.
- Whether a compact can substitute in the other direction, broadening what it enforces past
  what it recites, and whether that is a failure at all or the ordinary way a covenant
  learns what it meant.
