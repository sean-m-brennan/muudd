# Sacrificial equilibrium: the member the compact will not judge

## Thesis

A community whose internal crises resolve through the selection of a victim can hold full
membership in a kith-covenant compact. It satisfies the substrate requirement, it can
present all five anti-spiral features in good faith, and every continuous instrument the
compact owns reads it as healthy, because the mechanism works. The compact forms no verdict
on that interior, for the reasons
[natural_law.md](natural_law.md#the-interior-of-a-member-community) sets out, and this
document is not an argument for revisiting them.

What the compact owes itself in that situation is discipline of its own conduct. Sacrifice
propagates, and the channels it propagates through are the compact's own: the boundary
where the compact supplies parties, the records that cross between members, and above all
the designation apparatus, which is a legitimated procedure for selecting a victim and
would be an instrument of extraordinary power in the hands of a member that thinks in those
terms. The measures set out below are all measures on the compact and on what crosses
between members. None of them is a sanction on the member, and the difference is the whole
of the design.

## What a sacrificial equilibrium is

Girard's account holds that desire is borrowed from others, that rivalry follows from
communities sharing a field of desire, and that archaic societies resolved the resulting
crises by converging on a victim whose expulsion restored peace. The mechanism works
through misrecognition. It is efficacious precisely because the participants experience it
as justice rather than as arbitrary selection, and the peace that follows is real, which is
what recommends the mechanism to the next generation.

A community in equilibrium with that mechanism is not a community in crisis. This is the
distinction that matters for the compact, and it is the distinction the corpus's existing
instruments were not built to draw. Salem is an acute spiral: sixteen months, out of
equilibrium, visible from outside as a spike in every measure the compact watches, and
terminated from within its own substrate. A mature sacrificial cycle is the opposite
condition. The rite arrives on a schedule, the crisis it answers is periodic, and the
community's own account of the practice is that it is what keeps the peace. In Girardian
terms the equilibrium case is the older and more stable arrangement, and the spiral is what
happens when the mechanism fails.

## Why admission goes through

The compact's criteria are not silent here. `ScapegoatDisarming` is one of the five
required anti-spiral features, and the credentialing examination looks at operative
practice rather than confessed doctrine. Admission goes through anyway, for three reasons
that hold together.

**The verdict is recorded, not adjudicated.** The protocol records each community's feature
claims and each accepting community's response to them, and declines to rule on substrate
truth. That is architecture decision 6 and it is load-bearing for every other member.
Evidence for a claim may be the community's own sealed rite or a bare declaration.

**The claim is coherent from inside.** Asked how its substrate supplies scapegoat-disarming,
such a community answers that its rite is the terminator: tension arrives, the rite
discharges it, peace follows, and the community can point to a long record of that peace.
It is not lying, and the examination it faces has no way to reach past the account to the
thing itself without the adjudication the protocol refuses.

**The Salem proxies do not catch it.** Those proxies are purge-and-replace in place of
restoration, a legible competitive-sanctity economy, and mistreatment of designated
outsiders. An equilibrium has none of those signatures. The rite *is* the restoration path.
The status economy is quiet, because the discharge is scheduled rather than competitive.
The internal dispute rate, which the corpus names as what a spiral looks like from outside,
reads low, because the mechanism works. On the instruments the compact actually owns, such
a member reads healthier than an honest member with live internal dissent.

The durability argument compounds this rather than correcting it. Sacrifice is among the
most transmission-proven institutions in the human record, so a filter that selects for
what lasts ranks it high. That is the survivorship objection in its sharpest form, and the
answer to it is in [natural_law.md](natural_law.md#the-interior-of-a-member-community)
rather than here.

## How sacrifice reaches the rest of the compact

Four vectors, in order of what the compact can do about them.

**Apparatus capture.** A member that thinks in victims does not need to export its rite. It
needs the compact's designation machinery, which is a procedure for selecting a party,
declaring it destructive, and cutting it off, carrying the legitimacy of the whole compact.
The four-condition test bounds it heavily, and one of those conditions is compact-wide
deliberation with published reasoning, which is unanimity-minus-one with an archive. This is
the vector with the most leverage and the most available remedy.

**Inversion.** The compact turns on the member. Hunting a scapegoater is the purest
available form of the thing being hunted, and it is the vector that would make the compact
itself the carrier. It is also the most likely vector, because it requires nothing of the
member and only sincerity from everyone else.

**Boundary predation.** Girard's selection rule prefers victims with no avengers, and the
compact's frontier manufactures exactly that population: parties encountered in trade or
transit, travelers under passage, the rescued, envoys at liminal rendezvous, and machines
whose only avenger is one named guardian.

**Demonstration.** Other members observe that one member resolves crisis cheaply and
durably while their own crises cost mediation cycles and restitution. This is mimesis of
the resolution and requires no contact at all.

## What the compact does

### The boundary is closed

No compact channel supplies a victim to a member that asks for one. A person or machine
that arrived through a compact channel is within the order by that arrival, and the
forbearance owed is the same forbearance owed to any party the compact touches. This is
already the compact's ground rather than a measure adopted for this case
([natural_law.md](natural_law.md#the-ground-the-compact-claims-for-itself), site 4), and it
is the one commitment on which membership here is genuinely conditional. The condition is
on the boundary and not on the practice.

Supplying a victim on request is conduct directed at the compact's premise rather than at
an outcome within it, which is the category condition 1 of the designation test already
recognizes.

### Compact-facing records carry the other account

A record that crosses between members and cites a resolution-by-expulsion must carry the
expelled party's own account, or an attested null recording that the account was sought and
was refused or unavailable. Carried and not cited, because a citation dangles the moment
the archive prunes, which is the lesson the polity tier learned about backing evidence.

This is a rule about compact records and not an inspection of anyone's interior. Its
purpose is the demonstration vector: what propagates is the result stripped of the cost,
peace restored with the arbitrariness of the selection removed, and reattaching the
arbitrariness is what disarming the mechanism means in Girard's own account. The compact
cannot prevent members from observing each other and should not try. It can refuse to be
the medium through which the observation arrives pre-laundered.

### The designation apparatus cannot reach unanimity

Two amendments to the procedure, both on the compact and neither on any member.

An advocate is drawn by lot from a community that is not a party, with the duty of stating
the strongest case for the designated party, and the statement is archived as part of the
designation record. A designation that draws no dissent at all triggers mandatory
re-examination rather than counting as confirmed: unanimity is read as an alarm and not as
a warrant. The rule is the Sanhedrin's on unanimous capital verdicts, and its structural
effect is that all-against-one is not a state the compact can enter.

This is the answer to apparatus capture and to inversion at the same time, which is why it
belongs here rather than in either treatment separately. A compact that cannot converge on
a single victim is proof against a member that would use it to find one, and against
itself when the member becomes the obvious candidate.

A third constraint on the same apparatus is already in force and belongs beside these. The
designation procedure's emergency variant relaxes the requirement for a sustained pattern,
and the covenant-facing floor holds that it may not thereby rest on nothing: an expedited
designation must cite an attested adverse act by the subject, so the shortened route still
begins from conduct that reached the covenant rather than from an account of what a
community does at home. The specification is in
[protocol-components.md](../architecture/protocol-components.md) §5. That floor and these
two amendments are the same commitment approached from opposite ends, one bounding what a
designation may rest on and the other bounding how the compact may agree about it.

### The perimeter measure

An absolute measure, per member, of whether the population bearing its internal sanctions
coincides with the population holding no channel of appeal. Absolute rather than
act-triggered, because a member in equilibrium does nothing that trips an act-triggered
alarm. A lens and never a gate.

The ground for taking this measure at all is the capacity distinction rather than any moral
one. A member whose protective perimeter is far narrower than the compact's is a member
that will produce boundary incidents, because the compact's frontier keeps presenting it
with parties who fall outside that perimeter and inside the compact's. What the measure
predicts is compact-facing conduct, and the compact is entitled to predict compact-facing
conduct. It is not entitled to score the arrangement, and the lens does not.

This is the measure closest to the line, and it should be read as sitting on it. The
protection is that it produces no authority, gates no act, and reaches no verdict.

## What the compact does not do

The obvious measure is to admit such a member to every relation of exchange and bar it from
every office that selects who is sanctioned: the mediator pool, the observer corps, the
examining seats, deliberative weight in a designation, a seat on an emergency injunction
panel. The compact cannot express this today, since its capability vocabulary covers
services and not offices, and it should not adopt it on this ground even once it can.

An office bar triggered by a member's interior practice is a sanction premised on precisely
the verdict the compact declines to form. It would be the judging posture installed as
procedure, and it fails in the way the judging posture always fails: it makes the compact
the arbiter of its members' inner lives, which converts membership into submission and
hands whoever holds the machinery a respectable instrument for finding a member deficient.
The measure would also work, which is what makes it dangerous. Effectiveness is not the
test.

Office capabilities remain worth building for covenant-facing triggers, and that is filed
as such. The trigger may not be the interior.

## What none of this reaches

The demonstration vector survives. A member that resolves its crises cheaply is visible to
its neighbors, the visibility is not something the compact can or should suppress, and the
carried-account rule raises the cost of the demonstration without removing it. If the
mechanism spreads by imitation, it spreads, and what stands against it is each member's own
substrate and the anti-spiral features that substrate carries. The compact affords the
conditions and does not produce the resistance.

This is the same limit the corpus states everywhere else about what protocols can do, and
it should not be softened here because the case is vivid.

## Open questions

- Whether the carried-account rule is gameable by a member that produces an account from
  the expelled party under conditions the compact cannot see. The attested null is honest
  about absence; it says nothing about a statement that exists and was procured. A rule
  that improves the record while degrading its reliability would be worse than no rule.
- What the advocate drawn by lot is owed by way of protection. The role attracts exactly
  the hostility the observer corps and jester function are built to survive, and it is
  filled by a community rather than by an office-holder with those protections.
- Whether the perimeter measure can be computed at all from what the compact holds. It
  needs the shape of a member's internal sanctions and the shape of its internal appeals,
  and a member is under no obligation to make either legible.
- Whether an equilibrium is stable in contact with a compact that refuses it victims. The
  mechanism's demand does not disappear because the frontier closes, and where the demand
  goes instead is not something this document can predict.
