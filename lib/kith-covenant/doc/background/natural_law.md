# Natural law: the inheritance kith-covenant keeps and the method it refuses

## Thesis

Two accounts of an unlegislated standard bear on this design, and kith-covenant stands in
a different relation to each. The Thomist account derives a standard from what human
beings are and holds it binding on everyone; the compact keeps a great many of its
conclusions, receives them from the member communities that hold them, and asserts none
of them itself. The functional account derives a short list of forbearances from survival
plus the circumstances of vulnerable parties; the compact claims that list as its own
operative ground wherever it acts and consent has run out.

Hence three things: a silence, a commitment, and a limit. First, the phrase
"natural law" never appears in the voice of the compact itself, because a
protocol that named it as its foundation would have ruled on the nature of
the human person, which is the first-order metaphysical ruling the
substrate-deference test forbids. Second, the compact does not pretend to act
on nothing at its edges. The duties owed to a community that has signed
nothing, and the designation of an actor who has repudiated the frame
entirely, rest on the minimum content stated in [§"The ground the compact
claims for itself"](#the-ground-the-compact-claims-for-itself). Finally, that
ground is thin by design, and its limits carry as much weight as the ground
does. It reaches the compact boundary and the interior of no member.

A member community whose substrate is itself natural-law shaped, which in
practice means most Catholic and many Reformed and Islamic communities, should
know precisely what it is agreeing to and precisely what the compact declines
to say. It should know too where the compact leans on the account of the
community itself to carry a weight the protocol cannot carry.

## Two accounts of an unlegislated standard

### The Thomist account

Natural law holds that there is a standard of right action grounded in the nature of
things rather than in enactment, knowable by reason, and binding on everyone regardless of
what any legislator, custom, or majority says. Two corollaries travel with it. The
epistemic one: the standard is discovered, not invented, and practical reason reads it off
what human beings are and what fulfills them, the way theoretical reason reads off the
structure of the world. The juridical one: posited law derives its authority from
conformity to that standard, so a statute contradicting it is defective *as law* and not
merely as policy.

The move from principle to precept runs through three steps, each buying concreteness at
the cost of certainty. The first principle, that good is to be pursued and evil avoided,
is nearly empty and gives the shape of practical reasoning rather than its content.
Content arrives by asking what human beings are constituted to seek. Aquinas reads the
natural inclinations: to self-preservation, to the propagation and rearing of offspring,
to knowledge of the truth about God, and to life in society. Finnis's modern
reconstruction expands the same move into a list of basic Goods, each self-evidently worth
pursuing and none reducible to the others: life and health, knowledge, friendship and
sociability, marriage, practical reasonableness, aesthetic experience, play, and religion.
Congruent behavior participates in these Goods; incongruent behavior attacks one or more of
them directly or indirectly.

From the Goods come precepts in descending order of reliability. Primary precepts sit so
close to the Goods that no one sincerely misses them: do not kill the innocent, do not
take what is another's, do not bear false witness, keep faith with those who trusted you,
raise the children you beget, return good for good received. Secondary precepts follow by
short inference and are occasionally missed by whole cultures under distorting pressure.
Tertiary conclusions require long chains of reasoning, and honest people diverge on them;
most applied questions of political economy live there.

The tradition is far more confident about what may never be done than about what should be
done, and this asymmetry is where its concrete edge lives. Negative precepts bind *semper
et pro semper*, always and in every case, because refraining is always available.
Affirmative precepts bind always but not in every case, since feeding the hungry admits of
prudence about when, whom, and how much. The operational test follows: does this act
attack a basic Good directly, as the end or as the means? If yes, it is out whatever the
consequences. If no, the matter passes to prudence.

Where the derivation stops, positive law begins. Aquinas calls the remainder
*determinatio.* Natural law requires that traffic be regulated and does not say drive on
the right, requires that contracts be enforceable and does not specify the statute of
frauds. Positive law here is not deduced but freely specified within bounds the standard
sets, which is why natural law underdetermines political arrangements far more than either
its critics or some of its enthusiasts assume.

One further commitment matters for the comparison. The primary precepts
cannot be erased from the human heart. Although vicious custom and evil
persuasion blot out the secondary conclusions, and Aquinas supplies the
examples, *synderesis*, the habitual grasp of the first principles of
practical reason, persists under every corruption. Knowability, on this
account, is durable.

### The functional account

The functional account asks a smaller question and answers it without a
teleology. Given survival as an aim of the parties present, and a handful of
truisms about human vulnerability and approximate equality, and about
altruism, resources, and understanding all being limited, any viable order
among such parties contains a specific short list of forbearances. Hart's
minimum content of natural law is the canonical statement. The forbearances
are not read off human nature; they are what the circumstances of parties in
a shared predicament require of any arrangement that lasts among them.

Three features of the account matter as much as its conclusion. It is hypothetical rather
than categorical: it says what an order must contain if it is to persist, and says nothing
about whether persisting is good, what the human end is, or what anyone is owed in virtue
of what they are. It carries no juridical corollary of the Thomist kind, since an order
that omits the list is not defective *as law* but merely unlikely to survive. And it is
explicitly silent about extent. Hart's own text concedes that the forbearances need extend
only to those the order counts as within it, so a system that withholds them from a
subject class remains a functioning system. The account settles what is owed and never who
is counted.

### Where the two part company

The accounts diverge at four points, and the divergences are not vocabulary.
They part over truth, bearer, recourse, and direction.

*On what makes the standard true.* The Thomist standard holds whether or not
anyone recognizes it, because the teleology is real. The functional standard
holds among parties who happen to be vulnerable, matched, and generous only
sometimes, and it says nothing at all about parties differently situated.

*On who is bound.* Natural law binds persons directly and universally. The
functional account binds nobody in that sense; it describes what an arrangement
must contain, and its reach stops where the reckoning of an order itself of its
members stops.

*On what recourse is available when a standard and a practice conflict.* The Thomist has
an appeal: the derivation was faulty, and here is the correct one. The functional account
has no such appeal, and this is where its cost is concentrated. It cannot say that an
arrangement which has lasted got its content wrong, because lasting is the whole of the
evidence it consults.

*On the direction of the reasoning.* Natural law begins from what human beings are and
reasons forward to what may never be done to them. The functional account begins from what
durable arrangements in fact contain and stops there, offering no account of why those
contents rather than others are the right ones.

These are two different instruments, and kith-covenant uses each for the work it
can do. The Thomist account is content the compact receives from members and
never asserts. The functional account is the ground the compact claims for its
own conduct. Neither substitutes for the other: no functional argument produces
the anti-spiral features on which the whole design rests, and no derivation by a
member can be made to speak for the compact.

## Why the compact never names natural law

A protocol that named natural law as its foundation would be making a ruling
about the nature of the human person and the structure of the Good. Every
member community would then be joining a compact that had settled a question
its own substrate exists to answer. The compact-incapable list exists to
prevent exactly that. Theology, the internal governance of members, and the
content of recognition statements after formation are matters the compact
structurally will not decide, and a natural-law derivation of the common
virtues would violate the first of those directly. Aquinas's derivation runs
through an inclination to know the truth about God, and a Theravada member
community cannot receive that as the ground of its refusals without receiving a
theology it does not hold.

The absence is therefore a design consequence and not an omission. The material appears
once in the corpus, in the perennialist reading, where Justin Martyr's *logos spermatikos*
and Romans 1 and 2 on natural revelation and the law written on the conscience serve as
Christian resources for affirming that God works through other substrates. That is natural
law appearing as evidence inside a reading the corpus holds open, not as a premise the
compact adopts.

What the compact does with the derivation of a member is receive it. A Catholic
member community reads the common virtues as the primary precepts and is reading
them correctly by its own lights. The compact records that the community holds
the reading and endorses nothing, which is the treatment every substrate gets.

## Where the design arrives where the tradition arrives

### Non-ultimacy, recited in advance

Movement 5 of the recognition statement has each community declare that the
compact is not itself the ultimate, that its authority is contingent on
continuing to serve what it was formed to serve. It declares further that if
the compact becomes the captured instrument of any one community, of any
ideology, or of its own perpetuation, "we are not bound to it; the higher
commitments take precedence." That is *lex iniusta non est lex*, converted from
a thesis about the concept of law into a recitation every member has on the
record before any dispute arises.

The conversion improves on the tradition at one specific point. The classical formulation
tells you that an unjust law lacks authority. It does not tell you who notices, or when, or
what they do about it. Movement 5 assigns the noticing to every member as a condition of
membership, and it is the only Archimedean point the compact has from which to evaluate
its own decay. Salem is the calibrating case: a polity that held the anti-spiral features
in doctrine, spiraled anyway, and then self-terminated in about a year by appeal to an
authority above its own court. No featureless regime has produced that sequence.

### A list shaped like the primary precepts

The common virtues are reverence for life including the life of the
adversary, sanctuary of the rescuer, protection of the communicative
commons, and passage for the traveler. They are also innocence of the
non-combatant and the child, inheritance of the future, memory of the dead,
and good faith of the confession. While the overlap with the primary
precepts is heavy, it is not accidental. The argument for the list is the
natural-law argument in empirical dress. These are what culturally
unconnected communities converge on when they converge at all. The
thin-functional-domain cases (Kadesh, the Rhodian Sea Law, diplomatic
immunity arising independently in polities with no contact, the Geneva and
Red Crescent frameworks) are the evidence that the convergence is real
rather than an artifact of shared descent.

Two items on the list are not primary precepts in any classical formulation.
Inheritance of the future, with its claim that destruction of the knowledge
commons is long-delayed genocide, and memory of the dead are both duties
running to persons who do not currently exist or no longer do. Although the
tradition can reach them through the common good extended across
generations, it does not usually put them at the front. Kith-covenant does,
because its target setting is habitats where the archive is the only thing
connecting the dead to the unborn.

### Refusals, and the asymmetry of the negative

The virtue list is structured as virtue followed by refusals, and the refusals
are shape rather than limit. A party behaving in the spirit of a virtue
generates further refusals as circumstances arise, and a party gaming the list
stays inside the letter while violating the spirit, which third parties can
see. Strip the framing and the refusals are exceptionless negative precepts. No
attacks on life support. No interdiction of rescue vessels. No false-flag use
of rescue markings. No collective punishment. No burning of archives. Each
binds in every case, and each is satisfiable by refraining.

This is the asymmetry of the tradition reproduced without the vocabulary. The
compact is confident and specific about what must never be done and
comparatively unspecific about what must be done. The affirmative duties
(contribution to the commons, mediation in good faith, participation in
re-affirmation) are left to prudential determination by threshold and quorum
rather than stated as absolutes.

### Determination, fenced

The rungs of the escalation ladder, mediator terms, quorum thresholds,
reputation half-lives, and the light-lag decision classes are pure
*determinatio*. Each is a matter of number, cadence, and threshold rather
than of nature. Even if human nature settled everything else, nothing in any
account of it entails a one-year transition cycle for voluntary withdrawal
or any particular length of mediator term. What is unusual, and better than
the practice of the tradition, is that the boundary is fenced explicitly.
The compact-incapable list states in advance which matters are not subject
to determination by the compact at all, so the line between what is
specified and what is received is a published artifact rather than a matter
of ongoing dispute.

## Where the design departs

### Substrate affords emergence, it does not dictate content

This is the deepest divergence and the corpus states it as a principle in its own right.
The compact supplies conditions, protected time, protected roles, protected categories,
and a material floor, under which sacred-equivalent commitments *emerge* in the
communities that hold substrate, and it refuses to desecrate what emerges. It never
specifies what the emergent content must be. Rescue is the strongest candidate for sacred
status in the space setting, because every party is simultaneously potential donor and
beneficiary, and the compact still declines to decree its sacredness, protecting it as a
common virtue and letting the sacredness accrue through practice. A decreed sacredness
would be civil religion, which is the degenerate form.

Natural law is the opposite motion. Content is read off nature by practical reason, the
substrate grounds the standard, and reason discovers it. The tradition derives, then
protects what it has derived. Kith-covenant protects, then declines to look inside at what
has grown. The two produce similar lists while doing different things, and the difference
shows the moment the lists disagree.

The appeal of natural law runs to reason applied to nature and is available to
anyone, including a stranger to the community whose derivation is faulty. The
appeal of kith-covenant runs inside the member, to the authority that community
has named above itself and above its own process, which is what Movement 5 puts
on the record and what terminated Salem from within. The compact also holds two
resources that no reading by a member overrides: the refusals, which bind in
every case, and the minimum content, which grounds its conduct where consent has
run out. What the compact gives up, and gives up deliberately, is standing to
tell a member that the content which emerged in it is wrong.

### The precept form is given up on purpose

A rule is a line, and a line is a surface against which adversaries optimize. A
rule-framed prohibition holds only so long as the disposition persists upstream
of it, and when the disposition collapses the rule is honored in letter and
gamed in spirit, which is worse than no rule at all because it supplies cover.
Boundary commitments are therefore articulated as shared virtue-expressions
rather than as prohibitions, and the virtue frame turns gaming into
information. An adversary discovered optimizing against the letter is thereby
identified as not sharing the virtue, and the other members learn something
they needed to know.

Natural law has the virtues too, and no serious Thomist would concede that
precepts are the whole of it. But the jurisprudential edge of the tradition,
the part that lets you say a statute is defective as law, is propositional and
precept-shaped, and that is the edge kith-covenant files off. The treatment in
this corpus of neighbor-love makes the preference explicit. It reaches for
Aquinas's *New* Law, the grace of the Holy Spirit poured into the heart with
written precepts merely instrumental to that interior gift. It concludes that
the obligation is non-codifiable because it is indexed to the encounter, and
nothing indexed to the encounter can be fully specified before the encounter
happens. That is a choice of *Summa Theologiae* I-II qq. 106 to 108 over the
treatise on law fifteen questions earlier. Kith-covenant is a New Law project
wearing procedural clothes.

### Knowability is treated as perishable

Against *synderesis*, kith-covenant builds instruments. Ceremony cost is tracked across
re-affirmation cycles by attendance patterns, witness comportment, and substantive
response. Erosion legibility is a distinct concern with its own architecture document,
because a community that merely stops does not trip any act-triggered alarm. Sincerity
assessment, hollowing detection, and substrate adequacy are human-only work that no metric
catches, and hollowing, forms persisting while substance erodes, is the deepest threat the
compact faces and the one most resistant to procedural countermeasures.

None of this makes sense if the law written on the heart stays legible under corruption.
The design assumes the opposite: that a community can hold the whole inheritance in its
doctrine, recite it accurately, and have lost it, and that detecting this requires trained
observers with longitudinal exposure rather than any examination of what the community
professes. This is a genuine disagreement with the tradition about the durability of
practical knowledge, and kith-covenant takes the more pessimistic side. Salem earns the
pessimism: all five features held in doctrine and defeated in practice by
membership-gating, status anxiety, and the fusion of covenant and polity.

### The bearer is the community, not the person

The substrate claim is precise. Each member community must anchor itself to
something above itself, and the compact must never claim to be that thing.
Nothing is required of individual members, whose internal dissent is a matter
for their own polity unless it produces protocol violations, and nothing is
claimed about what binds the compact absent the anchoring of its members.
Natural law binds persons directly and universally, and would say the compact is
bound whether or not a single member has recited anything.

The relocation is what lets the compact stay silent about first-order theology
while still requiring something. It also makes the moral standing of the compact
derivative in a way the standing of the tradition is not. Kith-covenant needs
you to hold a substrate. Natural law needs the substrate to hold you.

### The load-bearing features are not natural

Two of the five anti-spiral features are grace over merit and
scapegoat-revelation. Neither is reason-accessible from an inspection of
human nature and its inclinations. Standing received rather than earned, and
a mechanism that disarms the reflex of resolving group anxiety by exiling
someone, are revealed content in the Christian case. In the Buddhist case
they are supplied by *anatta* plus universal compassion, which is doctrine
rather than derivation. Aquinas would file both under the New Law.

So the most distinctive causal claim of kith-covenant, that these five features
are what separates regimes that stop spiraling from regimes that do not, rests
precisely on the material natural law brackets out as beyond the reach of
unaided reason. The division of labor the tradition itself draws would agree
that these matter and would deny that they belong to natural law. This is not a
defect in either position. It is a clean statement of why kith-covenant could
not have been derived from natural law even if the compact were willing to make
the metaphysical ruling.

## The ground the compact claims for itself

The compact's own ground, wherever it acts and consent has run out, is the
functional minimum: the short list of forbearances that any viable order among
vulnerable, approximately equal, imperfectly altruistic parties contains. That
ground is not the authority of the compact itself. The compact holds real
authority over its members and holds it by their consent, which is exactly why
it cannot reach a party that never consented, and Movement 5 forecloses the
compact from being the sort of ultimate that could. It is not the substrate of
any member either, which the compact receives and does not speak for. It is what
an order among parties of this kind must contain in order to be an order at all.

The compact can claim this and could not claim a natural-law derivation, and
the difference is not one of degree. A derivation rules on what the human
person is and what fulfills it, which is the first-order metaphysical ruling
the substrate-deference test forbids. The minimum content rules on neither. It
takes survival as a contingent aim of the parties who are in fact present and
reasons about what their circumstances require. A member community may
therefore hold that the aim is a created good, a conventional preference, or a
delusion, and none of those readings changes what the compact does at its
boundary.

### The four sites

1. **Duties owed to non-members encountered.** A community met in trade,
   transit, rescue, or disaster receives courtesy, data, and rescue: the
   courtesy owed to anyone, navigational data where it is safety-relevant,
   and help if distress is present. None of that is reciprocal, and none of
   it is contingent on the recognition rite of the other party, its
   substrate, or its willingness to enter the frame. These are boundaries
   the compact draws on its own behavior rather than limits it imposes on
   others, and the minimum content supplies their content and not the
   decision to draw them. What is owed to a party the order counts is what
   any viable order among the vulnerable contains. That the compact counts
   the party in front of it is the commitment of the compact itself, carried
   by the substrates of its members and recorded in the four-category
   framework. What the ground removes is the idea that the duty is
   discretionary once counted, which is the form the colonial failure
   actually took. Not a denial that the colonized were vulnerable, but a
   treatment of the forbearance owed them as a courtesy the stronger party
   could withdraw.
2. **The *hostis humani generis* designation.** The designation condemns
   pirates and slavers by a standard they have never accepted and do not
   share, and this is where the ground does work nothing else can. Although
   the threshold is high, and anathema is the presumption with this category
   the rare exception, the designation reaches an actor who never joined.
   The designation names actors whose orientation is destructive of the
   premise of the compact, all of which bounds the category without saying
   what authority reaches an actor who never joined. The minimum content
   says it: the slaver stands under what any viable order among vulnerable
   parties contains, and the designation names conduct that no such order
   can absorb. The compact claims no jurisdiction it was never given. It
   recognizes a standard that does not run through the consent of anyone.

   The Schmittian escape is available here and is refused. One could hold that *hostis
   humani generis* is a defensive category rather than a moral verdict, the compact
   declining to extend the frame rather than claiming the pirate is bound. But
   `justus_hostis.md` departs from Schmitt at exactly this point, insisting that the friend
   and enemy distinction is morally subordinate to the prior category of neighbor, and
   `neighbor.md` grounds that priority in a command that refuses to fix the neighbor as a
   category settled in advance. With neighbor-status prior to compact-status, obligations to
   non-members cannot be treated as purely internal to the compact.
3. **What binds a restricted member.** A restricted member commits to the common
   virtues without naming a transcendent authority, and neither of the ordinary
   answers of the compact is available for what makes those virtues binding on
   it. Its own substrate cannot ground them, because it has declined to name
   one. The compact cannot ground them, because Movement 5 denies the compact
   that role. The minimum content grounds them: the restricted member is held to
   the thin part of the standard on the same ground that binds the conduct of
   the compact itself. It is not held to a lesser standard on a weaker ground,
   which is what makes the category coherent rather than a courtesy, and the
   thinness is also its ceiling. Nothing in the minimum supplies the anti-spiral
   features, which is why restricted membership remains restricted.
4. **The refusals at the boundary.** The refusals governing what the compact
   does to those outside it stand on this ground and not on the reading of any
   member. Among them: no compact channel supplies a victim to a member that
   demands one. A person or machine arriving through a compact channel is within
   the order by that arrival, and the forbearance is owed on the same terms it
   is owed to any party the compact touches.

### The four limits

The four bear on derivation, modality, reach, and endorsement.

1. **It does not derive the common virtues.** The virtues are received from
   member substrates, each community supplying its own account and the
   compact endorsing none. Reception, record, and forbearance are the whole
   of what the compact does with them. The minimum content is a floor under
   the conduct of the compact itself, not a derivation of the list, and
   nothing in it licenses the compact to derive anything. The
   compact-incapable list stands unchanged.
2. **It is hypothetical, not categorical.** It says what an order must contain
   if it is to persist among parties of this kind, and nothing about whether
   persisting is good, what the human end is, or what is owed to anyone in
   virtue of what they are. This is what keeps the ground clear of a smuggled
   teleology, and it is the honest statement of how little the ground carries.
   A member whose substrate holds that the good is what is commanded can accept
   the boundary conduct of the compact as prudent without conceding that
   prudence grounds obligation.
3. **It reaches the boundary and not the interior.** The minimum content
   settles what is owed and never who is counted. It cannot tell the compact
   whom a member counts as within its own order, and it cannot reach inside a
   member to correct the answer. It does not even tell the compact whom to
   count at its own frontier, which the compact settles by its own commitment
   (site 1). This is the same fact as Hart's concession that a system
   withholding its forbearances from a subject class remains a functioning
   system, and the corpus does not soften it.
4. **It binds without endorsement.** A ground that does not run through consent
   does not need assent in order to operate, which is an entailment of the kind
   of ground it is rather than a hedge against it. The slaver stands under the
   minimum content while rejecting it entirely, and nothing changes if a member
   rejects it too. Three relations stay distinct. Assent to the compact is what
   every member gives and what the recognition rite is for. Assent to the
   account the compact gives of why its own conduct is right is asked of
   nobody. The operation of the ground is independent of both. Member
   communities receive the statement the compact makes of its ground exactly as
   the compact receives their substrate readings, recorded and not ratified. A
   community that regards functional groundings as a category error remains a
   full member in good standing, and its envoys are not asked to affirm a
   jurisprudence at the recognition rite. The restricted member of site 3 is
   bound in the same manner, by a standard that runs through nobody's
   agreement, its own consent to the compact being a separate fact about a
   separate thing. Requiring endorsement would exclude every strongly
   voluntarist substrate and would be the compact ruling on a jurisprudential
   question its members are entitled to answer for themselves, so this limit
   protects what limit 1 protects.

   The cost is that a ground nobody is asked to endorse has no constituency inside the
   compact. When the argument for narrowing the forbearance owed at the frontier arrives, no
   member holds the minimum content as its own commitment to defend. This is why the
   protection stated below is procedural rather than doctrinal: the asymmetric bar does the
   work that member conviction would otherwise do, because member conviction on this point is
   exactly what the compact declined to require.

### Interaction with the deference instruments

Two things are true of the minimum content and are easily run together. As a *standard* it
reaches parties who have consented to nothing, which is what site 2 rests on: the slaver
stands under it whatever he holds about it. As an *adoption* it is an act of self-binding,
the compact committing itself to act on that standard and acquiring no jurisdiction by
doing so. Recognizing a standard and holding a writ are different things, and only the
second would be a matter belonging to a member.

The minimum content is therefore not compact-incapable. It decides no matter
belonging to a member, and what the adoption binds is the conduct of the compact
itself, which is the one thing the compact has always been free to bind. Its
content is nonetheless exposed to the same pressure every other restraint
carries, and the pressure runs one way: an argument for narrowing the
forbearance owed at the frontier is always available and always pragmatic.
Contracting the minimum content therefore carries the asymmetric higher bar that
`capture_resistance.md` specifies for contracting the compact-incapable list,
and the contraction is observable on the same terms, which is what makes it a
capture signal rather than a policy adjustment.

The protocol carries this as its own artifact rather than as a note about the
list. The adoption is a singleton, its forbearances are keyed to the four sites
above, and a narrowing is refused unless it carries the contraction quorum. A
founding forbearance is contractible at that bar and not immutable, which is
the procedural shape limit 4 asks for. The bar does the work member conviction
would otherwise do, and it does that work by making the narrowing dear and
legible rather than by declaring it impossible.

## The sincere materialist, where the frameworks stop overlapping

The sincere materialist is the sharpest test the corpus poses, and it splits along a line
natural law would not draw.

Open materialism, a community holding materialist views while acknowledging the
limits of materialism and willing to act as if there could be more, can recite
Movements 2 through 4 honestly and may be admitted as a restricted member. Such
a member commits to the virtues, acknowledges its own fallenness, and
recognizes the other, without naming a transcendent moral authority it does not
believe in. Closed materialism, a community asserting materialism as the final
word and holding the theistic claims of other communities simply false, cannot
honestly recite Movement 3, because recognition of the other requires treating
the substrate of the other as bearing the same structural role. Such a
community is not capable of membership, and the compact says so rather than
softening the incompatibility.

A natural-law theorist answers differently. That community is bound anyway and
can in principle know the primary precepts by reason without the theism, which
is what Romans 2 asserts and what the whole claim of the tradition to
universality requires. It is also, awkwardly, what the perennialist reading the
corpus quotes most favorably would suggest.

The divergence is exact. Kith-covenant locates the requirement in the operative
relation of the community to a standard rather than in the truth of the
standard. The compact does not ask whether the closed materialist is right. It
observes that a community holding that position cannot perform the recognition
act, and excludes on capability rather than on error. That is a defensible
protocol decision. It is not a natural-law decision, and it is the point at
which the two frameworks stop being different vocabularies for the same thing.

## The interior of a member community

The survivorship objection is the strongest charge of the tradition and it lands
here. Convergence evidence establishes that the surviving regimes shared certain
features. It does not establish that those features are right, only that they
are durable, and durability is what any survivorship filter selects for. A
tradition of human sacrifice with excellent transmission properties passes the
same filter. Against a functional grounding the objection bites hardest, because
once the argument for the virtues is that anchored regimes last, nothing in the
argument says a durable regime got the virtues wrong. Natural law has that
resource and pays for it with a metaphysical commitment the compact does not
make.

At the boundary the charge has an answer. A regime that preys on the vulnerable
where the conduct of the compact itself is engaged is wrong on a ground any
viable order contains, and the compact can say so without borrowing a
metaphysics it has declined.

In the interior the compact has no answer, because it asks no question. **The
compact forms no verdict on the internal morality of a member community until
that morality touches the covenant.** Conduct toward another member, conduct
at the boundary, and conduct toward a party that arrived through a compact
channel all touch it. So does what the envoys of a member carry into compact
business, and so does a hollowing that its own re-affirmation cannot survive.
These are the concern of the compact because they are the concern of the
covenant. What a community does inside itself, until it reaches one of those,
is not a question the compact has deferred pending better instruments. It is
not the question of the compact.

One distinction keeps this from colliding with the instruments the compact
does run inward. Ceremony-cost tracking, hollowing detection, the
interpretive-plurality requirement, and the erosion measures all look inside
a member, and none of them is a moral audit. What they measure is the
**capacity of a member to remain a member**. The questions are whether the
substrate is still live, whether the rite still costs the community anything,
whether it can still re-affirm honestly, and whether one faction has cornered
the reading of its own substrate. That is a question about the covenant
relation, and the compact is entitled to it, because a member that can no
longer carry the relation is a fact about the relation rather than a fact
about the soul of the member. Whether what the community holds is good is the
other question, and the compact does not ask it.

The reason is not modesty about the instruments, though they are in fact
weak here. A mature sacrificial cycle reads healthy on every continuous
indicator the compact owns, because the mechanism works: internal dispute
rates fall, the rite is a documented restoration path, and the community can
present every anti-spiral claim in good faith by its own lights. The reason
is that a compact which appoints itself judge of member interiors has
claimed a superiority over them, and that claim is poisonous to the
cooperation the compact exists to carry. It does damage three ways at once.
It converts membership into submission, which makes the compact the ultimate
and is exactly what Movement 5 forecloses. It installs the reading of the
compact itself as the standard, which is the derivation the
compact-incapable list forbids. And it hands whoever holds the deliberative
machinery a legitimate instrument for finding a member deficient, which is
the road the anathema safeguards are built against. The judging posture is
itself the spiral posture: a body that has appointed itself arbiter of the
interiors of others has already begun selecting victims.

So the boundary is not a limitation grudgingly conceded. It is the right place
for the line. The functional silence of the functional ground about who an order
counts and the refusal of the compact to judge what it would find there are two
different reticences, and they stop at the same line. What the compact watches
is effects, and effects are legible in a way interiors are not. A member whose
interior practice has consequences for the covenant produces those consequences,
and the escalation ladder, the four-category framework, and the continuous
instruments all operate on them.

The cost is real and is not hidden. The compact can carry a member whose
interior life it would not defend, and it does so knowingly. It holds that the
alternative, a compact confident enough to audit the souls of its members, fails
sooner and worse. What the compact owes itself in that situation, which is
discipline of its own conduct rather than judgment of the member's, is worked
out in [sacrificial_equilibrium.md](sacrificial_equilibrium.md).

## What kith-covenant supplies that the tradition does not

The tradition has centuries of work on what the standard is and comparatively
little on what happens when the community carrying it hollows out while its
forms stay intact. That failure is the central preoccupation of kith-covenant
and the thing it built instruments for.

The purity spiral is named as a specific failure mode with causes that are
structural, statable, and specific, and the five features supply termination
and repair rather than prevention, which is the more honest and more useful
claim. Ceremony cost decay is tracked as a quantity, sampled for time,
attention, and substance. Erosion is made legible by absolute measures
rather than act-triggered ones, because a polity that merely stops trips
nothing. The jester is promoted from cultural affordance to substrate-level
capture detector, and is protected, insulated, and equipped: institutionally
protected, structurally insulated by rotation and plurality, and given
technical channels including ring-signed whistleblower attestation.
Interpretive plurality is a structural requirement on members, so that no
faction monopolizes the reading of its own substrate.

And the collusion threshold is stated as a limit rather than buried. Past
roughly a third of the compact coordinating while holding the mediator pool,
observer corps, archive, and deliberative body, the architecture becomes an
instrument of the faction. No historical regime has solved this, and the
compact survives only through substrate-level renewal that the protocol can
afford conditions for and cannot itself produce. The tradition has nothing
comparable, because it has never had to specify a protocol and therefore has
never had to state where its protocol fails.

## What follows for the design

1. **No natural-law derivation in the compact.** It would be the first-order metaphysical
   ruling the substrate-deference test forbids, and it would exclude the Buddhist and
   Haudenosaunee cases the corpus relies on as evidence.
2. **Receive the natural-law reading when a member offers it.** The compact records that
   the community holds the reading and endorses nothing, which is the same treatment every
   other substrate gets.
3. **Where consent has run out, the ground is the functional minimum.** The
   duties owed to non-members encountered, the ground of the *hostis humani
   generis* designation, what binds a restricted member, and the refusals at the
   boundary all rest there rather than on the authority of the compact or on the
   substrate of a member. This is the smallest commitment that supports the
   practice, and it is not theology. Its four limits travel with it wherever it
   is stated, and the third especially: the ground reaches the compact boundary
   and grants no writ inside a member.
4. **No verdict on the interior of a member until it touches the covenant.**
   Effects are the trigger, and the compact instruments are pointed at effects.
   A compact that judges interiors claims a superiority that corrodes the
   cooperation it exists to carry.
5. **Keep the virtue frame as the operative form and let the refusals carry the precept
   work.** The refusals already function as exceptionless negative precepts. Nothing is
   gained by relabeling them and the gaming resistance of the virtue frame would be lost.
6. **Do not import the durability of** synderesis. The instruments for hollowing exist
   because the design assumes practical knowledge perishes, and a member community whose own
   tradition holds that the primary precepts cannot be erased should understand that the
   compact will still measure its ceremony cost. That measurement is of capacity and not of
   morality, which is what keeps it clear of item 4.

## Open questions

- Where the line falls between an interior matter and one that touches the
  covenant. Both ends are clear and the middle is a judgment. The treatment by
  a member of its own people reaches the covenant when a party that arrived
  through a compact channel is drawn into it, when it appears in the conduct of
  an envoy, or when the community can no longer re-affirm honestly. Which
  further effects count is not fully specifiable in advance, and a stated
  judgment call is preferable to a false precision that an adversary would
  optimize against.
- Whether the third limit survives its first hard case. That limit holds that
  the ground reaches the compact boundary and not the interior of any member,
  and the rule of the compact itself holds that no verdict is formed on an
  interior until it touches the covenant. The case that tests both is the one
  where the internal conduct of a member is plainly visible to the rest of the
  compact, plainly grave, and plainly outside anything the compact is entitled
  to judge. Nothing in the design is wrong on that occasion, and every member
  will want it to be. The pressure will be to find a reading of "touches the
  covenant" wide enough to reach the conduct, and it will come from the members
  with the best motives, which is what makes it dangerous rather than merely
  difficult.

  A second difficulty compounds the first. Because the compact watches effects rather than
  interiors, the earliest moment it can act is the moment an interior practice produces a
  compact-facing effect. By then the practice is established, and whatever it has done to
  the people inside that community has been done. This delay is a property of the rule and
  not a shortcoming of the instruments, and no sharpening of the instruments removes it.
  What the corpus does not know is whether the delay stays acceptable once it is measured
  in a particular case against particular people.
- Whether the two additions to the classical list, inheritance of the future
  and memory of the dead, are derivable in the terms of the tradition itself
  from the common good extended across generations. And what it would mean for
  the convergence argument if they turned out to be specific to closed-system
  habitation rather than general.
