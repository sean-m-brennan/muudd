# Capture resistance

The same machinery that makes this compact work makes it capturable. Whoever
controls the mediator pool, the audit commons, the admissions gate, or the
interpretation of the substrate controls the compact.

That is not a discrete failure to be prevented. It is continuous structural
pressure, always operating, never finished. Treating capture as an event that
either has or has not happened is the mistake that lets it happen, because the
event never arrives; what arrives is a slow accumulation of ordinary decisions
that each looked reasonable.

Seven moves keep the pressure bounded. None of them eliminates capture. They
bound the damage and slow the spread, and together they raise the threshold at
which a faction becomes decisive.

## The seven moves

*Interpretive plurality.* Member communities must maintain multiple traceable
lineages of their own substrate, so that no single faction may monopolize
interpretation. A community with one interpreter has already handed somebody the
substrate.

*The substrate-deference test.* The compact maintains a *compact-incapable list*
of matters it structurally will not decide, being theology, the internal
governance of members, and the content of recognition statements after formation,
together with a *structured-fast cadence* of periodic intervals during which
compact-level mediation is suspended. Both operationalize the claim that the
compact is not the highest thing, and the fast does so by making the compact
periodically stop, which is a thing an ultimate cannot do.

*Ceremony-cost tracking.* Recognition rites that have decayed into ritual without
engagement are detectable by attendance patterns, by the comportment of
witnesses, and by substantive response during re-affirmation cycles.

*Manufactured-event resistance.* High-interpretive-charge events, meaning an
incident that conveniently justifies escalation, trigger structured delay,
multi-witness corroboration, cross-substrate translation, and stake disclosure
before any binding response.

*Light-lag-honest decision classes.* Cryptographically distinct classes for local
operational choices, valid within light-lag, and compact-level decisions
requiring quorum signatures from communities at multiple distances. Attempts to
reclassify a second-class decision as first-class are themselves second-class
decisions, which makes the router self-protecting and forecloses the fait
accompli.

*Adversarial-principal validation.* Cross-community witnesses observe the conduct
of each principal, behavior is triangulated against the ensemble of
similarly-situated peers, and divergence in directions that benefit the principal
is flagged.

*Admissions integrity.* The gate is the slowest capture surface, because steering
admissions is the legitimate path to assembling a coordinated faction past the
collusion threshold. Credentialing panels are drawn by lot and rotated like the
mediator pool. Admission is a multi-community quorum decision. The standing of a
sponsor rides on the later conduct of the entrant. The observer corps watches
admission patterns for factional clustering. And probationary members carry no
deliberative weight in admissions or designation.

## Non-ultimacy as a constitutional artifact

The second move deserves its own treatment, because it is the one that keeps the
compact from becoming the thing the whole design exists to avoid.

The compact-incapable list is a genesis-immutable constitutional artifact with
*asymmetric amendment*. Expanding it is easy. Contracting it is harder.

The asymmetry is the mechanism rather than a stylistic preference, and the
reasoning is worth stating because it recurs at every tier of this stack. The
argument for dropping a restraint is always available. There is always a crisis
in which the restraint is inconvenient, and by construction the crisis is
precisely when the restraint matters. What answers an argument that is always
available is an asymmetry rather than a prohibition, since a prohibition invites
the argument that this case is different.

The thin functional minimum the compact adopted as the ground of its conduct
toward parties that have not joined is *not* on the compact-incapable list, and
the reason is the reason the list exists. The list names matters belonging to
*members* that the compact will not decide. The minimum decides nothing belonging
to a member; it restrains the conduct of the compact and grants no writ inside
anybody.

Because it is a restraint on the compact, contracting it carries the same
asymmetric higher bar as contracting the list, and the contraction is a capture
signal on the same terms. Both are artifacts and both bars are rules. The one
difference is that a *founding* forbearance yields at the higher bar where a
founding compact-incapable matter is immutable.

Interim injunctions and capability gating sit alongside this, so that something
can be stopped immediately without anybody acquiring a standing power to stop
things.

## The failure with no adversary

Six of the seven moves above assume somebody is doing something. That assumption
turned out to be the largest hole in the whole apparatus, and finding it required
looking for a failure nobody causes.

A compact does not have to be captured to stop working. It can simply stop.
Attendance thins, re-affirmations lapse, offices sit empty, and the observer
corps quietly stops filing. Nothing was decided, so nothing was recorded, and
every act-triggered detector reads clean.

Measuring that is what showed the size of it. The erosion alert was three for
three act-triggered, so a polity that merely *stopped* scored nothing at all. A
re-examination that had been confirmed read identically to one that had been
neglected. An office empty for forty-three years read clean on every instrument
the compact had. And one cadence was unreachable by construction, because the
term limit retired the seats before the interval elapsed.

The rule that comes out of it generalizes past this compact: **only absolute
measures see sincere drift.** A measure that fires on an act cannot see the
absence of acts. The alert now carries nine signals, six of them absolute,
including silent and unstaffed standing functions.

Two record types close the specific holes. An *attested null* is the signed
account by a community of one window in a standing function it holds, whose empty
act list is the point of the record. And a recorded *conclusion* of a mandated
re-examination distinguishes a confirmation, which answers its deadline and sets
the next one, from a lapse. A lapsed designation may not be cited by a further
escalation act.

Silence is now either evidence or a fact about the compact. Before, it was
missing data, and a quiet pool could not be told from a stopped one.

## Measuring whether any of this works

The seven moves are claims, and claims about adversarial robustness are worth
what their tests are worth.

A capture-resistance harness scores ten scenarios against falsification criteria
*stated in advance*. The ten are the seven strengthening moves, with the
admissions move counted twice, for who a faction can seat and for what a seated
entrant can carry, plus the two with no adversary at all, being the quiet polity
and the abandoned office.

Stating the criteria in advance is the part that matters. A harness whose success
condition is written after the run is a description rather than a test.

It runs continuously over a parameterized space with seeded search, directed
sweeps, and shrinking. That last apparatus was added for a specific reason:
running the harness two hundred times proved the scoring function was a constant.
It returned the same answer regardless of input, which meant it had been
measuring nothing. Parameter sweeps are what make a harness capable of
distinguishing a design that resists capture from a design that resists being
measured.
