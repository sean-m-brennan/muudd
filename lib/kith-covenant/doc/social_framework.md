# Social Framework for Space Habitats

*Stub document. Topic identified in the project mind map but not yet developed.*

## Overview

Sustainable space inhabitation requires social technologies as much as physical ones. This document will cover the construction and maintenance of civilizational commitment ("social will"), the five-role social model, mechanisms for enshrining dissent, and the social structures needed to prevent collapse in isolated communities.

## Open questions

- How is "social will" (sustained civilizational commitment to space inhabitation) built and maintained across generations?
- How does the five-role model (warrior, priest, scientist, merchant, worker) map to habitat social structure?
- What mechanisms enshrine dissent (the "jester role") as a social safety valve?
- How are power vacuums prevented in small, isolated communities?
- How do communities survive nihilism, ideological upheaval, or loss of purpose?
- What "necessary physical efficiencies" constrain social organization in habitats?
