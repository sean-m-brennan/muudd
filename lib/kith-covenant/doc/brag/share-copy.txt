Kith-Covenant.
Two communities with different gods, different languages and different loyalties, cooperating with no authority standing above either — the problem the Hittites and Egyptians already had in 1259 BCE, worked out as a protocol with fifteen Rust crates behind it.
Cooperation without a sovereign.
