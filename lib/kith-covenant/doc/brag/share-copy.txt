Kith-Covenant.
A compact between communities can fail without anyone capturing it. Rites lapse, offices sit empty, and every detector that waits for an act reads clean. So the compact measures against references it cannot move, signs for its silences, and scores that failure in a harness whose criteria are written before the run.
Cooperation without a sovereign.
