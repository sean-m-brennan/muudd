Chapter 17 — from cooperation to community
What the tier above presumes
The compact takes a community as its atomic actor, and quietly presumes four things about that actor.
Bounded It has a known membership.
Coherent It stands for a substrate and can speak through delegates.
Persistent It outlives its current members.
Agentive It can consent, be sponsored, and be held responsible as a unit.
A trust substrate produces none of the four directly: its groups and gateway subtrees are emergent topological clusters rather than named, root-anchored, covenant-bearing entities.
Ratify emergence, do not replace it
Impose a structure and the community becomes a diktat rather than a people. Relabel the cluster and the mask has no inside.
01 Emergence proposes the trust field, the cluster boundary, the earned reputations
02 The polity ratifies one signed charter: the roll, the substrate, the commitments, the offices
03 The compact recognizes a community identity, in vocabulary it already has
