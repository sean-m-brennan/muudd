Ethne.
Nine machines that reliably deal with each other are not a community. Ethne is the tier that closes the gap: it reads the emergent structure and offers it back to the members for deliberate ratification into a charter — a roll somebody signed, a substrate the polity stands under, offices it confers on itself, and a rite by which a member actually receives what the roll is a list of.
Emergence proposes, the polity ratifies, and the compact recognizes.
