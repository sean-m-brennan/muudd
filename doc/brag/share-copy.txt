MUUDD.
One solar relay on a ridge, built so that nobody has to climb up to it: sized against December, made of shelf parts on unlicensed spectrum, routing around a dead neighbor, restarting itself, and diagnosed from a warm room. It belongs to the households it serves, and the tiers above it are optional.
Hardware that moves bits without asking permission.
