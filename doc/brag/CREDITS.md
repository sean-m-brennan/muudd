### The bed is filtered, not just ducked

At its own level this is an upbeat, bright, corporate-adjacent track, and it is
being asked to sit under a slow narration in a cinematic register. Ducking alone
was not enough — the hats and the sibilance sat right in the speech band, so the
bed argued with the voice even when it was quiet.

What plays in the composition is therefore a **low-passed copy**:

```bash
ffmpeg -y -i composition/assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 \
    -af "lowpass=f=2600" -c:a libmp3lame -q:a 3 \
    composition/assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app-lowpass.mp3
```

The 2.6 kHz corner takes the top off the hi-hats and the transient edge off the
plucks while leaving the pulse and the low end intact, which is all the bed is
being asked to carry. The **unfiltered original is kept beside it** in
`composition/assets/music/` — it is the file the bundled cue preset and
`hyperframes beats` were run against, and it is what anyone should start from to
re-derive the filtered copy.

With the filter in place the bed opens to **0.22** in the gaps rather than 0.30,
and still ducks to **0.13** under every voice span. Measured off the delivered
render: voice spans sit at ≈ −24.6 to −25.1 dB mean with peaks −5.2 to −6.9 dB,
the gaps between lines drop to ≈ −34 to −36 dB (bed only), so speech clears the
music by about **10 dB** throughout. The final fade reaches −46 dB.

`assets/audio-data.js` — the per-frame data the audio-reactive layer samples — is
extracted from the **filtered** copy, because that is what a viewer hears.
It covers the whole 60s track, so it needed no change when the cut grew to
49.5s on 2026-10-01. Regenerate it with:

```bash
python3 scripts/make_audio_data.py \
    composition/assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app-lowpass.mp3 \
    --output composition/assets/audio-data.js --seconds 60
```

## Narration

Synthesized voiceover, Kokoro `am_michael` at speed 0.96: **eight clips**
(`vo-1-hook` … `vo-8-lockup`, 43.09s of speech), generated on 2026-10-01 for the
re-concept into `composition/assets/voice/` via `hyperframes tts`. The seven clips
of the 2026-09-18 cut are kept in `previous-2026-09-18/composition/assets/voice/`
and are no longer referenced. These WAVs are **not**
regenerated by `build.sh` — the render deliberately skips the TTS backend — so
they are source, not build output.

The delivered audio was transcribed back with `hyperframes transcribe`
(whisper.cpp, `small.en`); every word of all eight lines came back, and the
lines with their measured durations are in `brag-plan.md` under "Voiceover
script". The delivered audio, not the written script, is the authority
for timing and for what the voice actually carries.
